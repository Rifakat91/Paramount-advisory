About Paramount

Organizations undertaking complex change deserve genuinely senior expertise, applied with rigour.

Paramount Advisory & Consulting is an advisory practice based in Mississauga, Ontario. We provide senior-level strategy, operations and program advisory to non-profit organizations, government bodies and large enterprises, and we pair that advisory work with research, capacity building and professional learning.

Our areas of expertise are strategy, operations, program and policy advisory, and organizational design. Whatever the sector, our commitment is the same: practical, evidence-informed solutions that organizations can put to work.

Vision
To be the advisory partner organizations turn to when a decision genuinely matters.
Mission
To connect organizations facing strategic, operational or programmatic challenges with sector-relevant expertise, matched precisely to the problem at hand.

Our approach

The right expertise, applied to the right problem, with rigour that holds across contexts.

  • Matched expertise

    Each mandate is shaped around the problem at hand, with sector-relevant expertise brought to it.

  • Rigour adapted to context

    A strategic plan for a hospital foundation and one for a municipal agency draw on the same discipline of method, adapted to each setting.

  • Built to be implemented

    Advice is only useful if it can be carried out, so we support change management and implementation, not only recommendations.

  • Measured by outcomes

    Performance frameworks, program evaluation and outcomes reporting keep the focus on what actually changes.

  • Professional conduct and confidentiality

    Our work is guided by shared standards of professional conduct and confidentiality, with additional data-handling protocols where a sector requires them.

Recent initiative · Paramount Learning Forum

Grant Writing & Proposal Development

A practical, self-paced professional development course for people working in, volunteering with, or preparing to enter the Canadian non-profit and charitable sector. It strengthens participants' ability to understand funding opportunities and to develop stronger grant proposals.

Register Now
  • Five modules from funding landscape to final application review
  • Self-paced with worksheets, templates and a final assessment
  • About 3.5 to 4.5 hours of recommended learning time
  • Certificate of Completion may be issued by Paramount. It is not an academic, government or regulated credential.
